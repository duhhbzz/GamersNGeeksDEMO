# Footer Specification

## Purpose

Provide secondary navigation, store information, community links, policies, and newsletter access.

## Required Content

- Logo
- Store address
- Store hours
- Phone or contact link
- Shopping links
- Event link
- TCG Singles link
- Social and community links
- Policy links
- Copyright notice

## Optional Content

- Newsletter form
- Map or directions link
- Accepted payment methods

## Mobile Behavior

- Sections may use accessible accordions
- Contact information remains immediately visible
- Touch targets meet accessibility guidance

## Accessibility

- Footer landmark
- Descriptive social link labels
- Proper form labels
- Logical heading hierarchy

## Acceptance Criteria

- Store location and hours are easy to find
- Policy and contact links remain available on all pages
