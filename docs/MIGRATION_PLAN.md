# 🚀 Shopify Migration Plan

---

# Purpose

This document outlines how the proposed redesign could be implemented while minimizing disruption to the existing Gamers N Geeks website.

The goal is to preserve existing business operations while modernizing the customer-facing experience.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Preserve existing Shopify infrastructure
- Avoid downtime
- Minimize business risk
- Maintain SEO
- Preserve existing URLs where possible
- Roll out changes incrementally

---

# Existing Infrastructure

Current platform components include:

- Shopify
- TCGPlayer Pro
- Shopify Apps
- Tockify Events
- Analytics
- Payment Processing
- Inventory Management

These systems should remain in place unless a compelling business reason exists to replace them.

---

# Migration Strategy

## Phase 1

Research

- Audit current theme
- Audit Shopify Apps
- Audit navigation
- Audit collections
- Audit product templates

---

## Phase 2

Prototype

Build an independent frontend demo.

No production changes.

No risk.

---

## Phase 3

Client Review

Gather feedback.

Refine layouts.

Adjust branding.

Finalize UX.

---

## Phase 4

Theme Development

Implement approved layouts inside Shopify.

Reuse as much backend functionality as possible.

---

## Phase 5

Testing

Desktop

Tablet

Mobile

Accessibility

Checkout

Search

Navigation

Events

Collections

---

## Phase 6

Launch

Deploy during low-traffic hours.

Monitor analytics.

Monitor performance.

Collect customer feedback.

---

# Data That Should Remain Untouched

- Orders
- Customers
- Inventory
- Products
- Variants
- Pricing
- Shipping
- Payment gateways

---

# Potential Risks

## Theme Compatibility

Mitigation:

Build on a duplicate theme.

---

## App Compatibility

Mitigation:

Test integrations individually.

---

## SEO

Mitigation:

Preserve URLs.

Preserve metadata.

Redirect only when necessary.

---

## Customer Familiarity

Mitigation:

Modernize gradually while preserving recognizable branding.

---

# Rollback Strategy

If issues occur:

- Restore previous Shopify theme.
- Verify data integrity.
- Review error logs.
- Resolve issues.
- Attempt deployment again.

---

# Success Metrics

- No downtime
- Improved Lighthouse scores
- Faster navigation
- Improved mobile experience
- Higher engagement with events
- Increased product discovery

The customer should experience a dramatically improved website without realizing the underlying business systems remained the same.
