# Success Criteria

The project is complete when:

- Users immediately understand what the store offers.
- Events are easy to discover.
- Shopping feels intuitive.
- The site performs well on mobile.
- The design reflects the quality of the physical store.
- The existing Shopify infrastructure remains intact.

# Guiding Principle

> Preserve what already works. Modernize everything the customer sees.
